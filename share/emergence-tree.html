How to read
  • Fundamentals are on the left, taken as given.
  • A node without a “theory only” or “simulation only” tag is demonstrated in a laboratory, seen in nature, or a mathematical identity. A tagged node rests on less; its panel says on what, with citations.
  • “Open in the field” marks a question the field has not answered; the panel says which.
  • A solid edge: the source physically gives rise to the target.
  • Yellow edges do not: dotted is a claim about validity, reach or impossibility; long-dashed is the same content re-described in a limit.
  • Orange: feedback.
  • Hide implied edges drops a solid edge when a longer solid path already joins the same two nodes. The node panel still lists every edge.
  • Zoom with − and +, Fit, or Ctrl+scroll.
  • Keyboard: Tab into the map, arrow keys move along edges and through a column, Enter selects, Escape clears.
  • Life and Earth are observations; the electronic Hamiltonian does not produce them here.
  • Incoming edges:
    • Produced by
    • A claim about
    • A limit of
    • Feeds back from
  • Outgoing edges:
    • Produces
    • Bounds
    • Its limits
    • Feeds back into
